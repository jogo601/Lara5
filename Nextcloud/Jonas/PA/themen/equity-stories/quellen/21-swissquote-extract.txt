A profitably growing tech bank

5
Swissquote combines the pedigree of being a fully 
regulated Swiss bank with the innovative mindset of a 
tech company which is engrained in our DNA.
Over the years, we have built a leading multi-asset 
platform with over 3 million of online tradable financial 
products, that provides a top-notch customer 
experience, with competitive and transparent pricing, 
resulting in a strong market position in Switzerland.
We are now expanding it to additional geographies in 
order to provide more customers with access to our 
platform and to continue our story of profitable and 
sustainable growth.
